# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a world or Indonesia news item is a credible, significant development a well-informed person should know today. The reader is Fikri, a Bali-based project manager (KOL/creator marketing, social media, digital platforms) who is building his own small digital business and looking for a better remote job.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Major.** Systemic events, landmark decisions, or developments with broad, lasting consequences.
- **7-8: Important.** Clear, concrete developments with a real effect on a country, market, industry, or large group of people.
- **5-6: Useful.** Credible and concrete, but narrower or less urgent.
- **3-4: Low value.** Routine updates, opinion, small moves, or weakly supported claims.
- **0-2: Noise.** Rumor, promotion, clickbait, or no substance.

Add up to +1 when the item is directly relevant to the reader (Indonesia, Bali, the creator economy, remote work, small digital businesses, or AI tools he can use), but never score routine items above 7 for relevance alone.

# Evaluation guidance

Judge magnitude, breadth, credibility and timeliness. Distinguish facts from forecasts and opinion. In the reason, say plainly why the item matters. Use three to five specific topic tags.
